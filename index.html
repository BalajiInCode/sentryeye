THE TRADITIONAL SURVEILLANCE PARADOX

"Normal CCTV only records.
It cannot stop anything."

Campuses worldwide deploy thousands of digital cameras, yet almost all of them remain passive witnesses to preventable emergencies.

VULNERABILITY 01

Fire Goes Unnoticed

Conventional CCTV lacks thermal awareness. Electrical short circuits and laboratory flare-ups emit smoke and high heat for critical minutes before anyone notices a flicker on crowded security monitors.

Consequence: Delayed emergency response and catastrophic facility damage.
VULNERABILITY 02

Camera Can Be Covered or Removed

Vandals and intruders can simply spray-paint lenses, drape cloth over domes, or physically twist camera mounts. Legacy systems register no immediate alarm, leaving fatal security blindspots undetected.

Consequence: Zero warning when intentional blindness or hardware theft occurs.
VULNERABILITY 03

Manual Monitoring is Tiring

Human attention drops quickly during long monitoring shifts, so important events can be missed.

Consequence: Critical incidents go completely unseen until post-event inquiry.

INNOVATION METHODOLOGY

The SCAMPER Framework

How we reimagined everyday campus CCTV from the ground up using systematic creative product innovation principles.

S
SUBSTITUTE

AI Monitoring Instead of Manual Watching

Replaces error-prone, sleep-deprived human monitoring walls with on-device computer vision models running 24/7. Detects loitering, stampedes, and unauthorized access instantaneously without human fatigue.

Autonomous Edge-AI Inference
C
COMBINE

Camera Vision + Thermal Sensing + Tamper Protection

Unifies three distinct defense layers into a single smart node: high-resolution optical video for visual clarity, thermal infrared micro-bolometers for early flame ignition, and internal 6-axis accelerometers for anti-tamper shock sensing.

Tri-Sensor Data Fusion
A
ADAPT

AI Facial-Recognition Attendance

Adapts security vision networks to tackle daily academic logistics. When mounted in lecture halls and classroom entrances, SentryEye automatically records student attendance with sub-second biometric verification, eliminating physical queues.

Zero-Touch Academic Logging
M
MODIFY

Ring of LED Lights Around the Lens

Features an integrated circular array of dual-spectrum LEDs surrounding the lens chassis. Delivers 360-degree uniform illumination for nighttime clarity and doubles as an emergency high-visibility warning strobe during campus lockdown alerts.

Wide Night Coverage & Active Strobe
P
PUT TO ANOTHER USE

Optional Metal-Sensor Module

Expands beyond optics through an auxiliary snap-on expansion bay. When deployed at exam hall entrances or hostel turnstiles, it connects to an electromagnetic sensor to detect concealed contraband, weapons, or unauthorized electronic devices.

Multi-Threat Perimeter Screener
E
ELIMINATE

Single PoE Cable for Power and Data

Eliminates separate DC transformers, battery maintenance hassles, and tangled multi-wire setups. A single standardized Power-over-Ethernet (802.3at PoE+) RJ-45 cable simultaneously supplies high-speed gigabit telemetry and operating voltage.

Zero Cable Clutter • Rapid Install
R
REVERSE

Rotating/Movable Camera (Drone Style as Future Scope)

Inverts the traditional concept of static, immovably bolted wall cameras. In our future architectural roadmap, SentryEye mounts on dual-axis magnetic tracks or autonomously disengages as a tethered micro-drone to fly directly to incident coordinates for 360-degree close-up inspection.

Autonomous Mobile Inspection Scope
